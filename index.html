Puzzle 001 · The locked library

Who took the key?

At 9:00 PM, a library key disappeared. Four people were questioned.

Aria: "I left at 8:30."
Ben: "I was in the garden from 8:45 to 9:15."
Clara: "I saw Ben inside the library at 9:05."
Dev: "The garden door was locked at 8:50."

The caretaker confirms the garden door can only be locked from inside the library corridor. Who is most suspicious?

Puzzle 002 · The three switches

Which switch controls the bulb?

You are outside a closed room. Inside is one old-fashioned light bulb. Outside are three switches; only one controls the bulb. You may operate the switches however you like, but you may enter the room only once. How can you identify the correct switch?

Puzzle 003 · The silent witness

Who is lying?

A valuable painting vanished from a gallery between 7:00 and 8:00 PM. The gallery clock had been removed for repairs that morning. The guard tells the detective: "I heard the gallery clock strike eight, so I know the theft happened after that." What makes his statement suspicious?
